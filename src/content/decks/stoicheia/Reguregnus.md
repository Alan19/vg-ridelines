Send your units to drop with your units' skills, and then revive them and power them up with "Call of Deep Lands"! Reguregnus rides into Reguregnus "Athanasios" after it attacks, then calling itself to a rear-guard circle and then gives "Athanasios" trigger effects it got on its own drive checks. Riding "Athanasios" allows you to call "Rotting Usurp Dragon" to get up to 6 attacks!
