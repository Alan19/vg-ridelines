Rush down your opponent with Genomegels and Ubermensch without losing much hand advantage, and then keep pressuring your opponent with Alchemix! Use Zorga's Fantome Skill and rear-guards' skills to replenish your board after they are consumed for Alchemix! Zorga also gets Triple Drive once you have bound 2 Crimson Moons!
