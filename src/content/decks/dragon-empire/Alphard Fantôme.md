Alphard gets more power the more damage you have, and gets critical if you use Fantome Skill! Bind 4 Crimson Moon orders when you Persona Ride to give Alphard a total of 10000 power, 2 criticals, 1 drive checks, and the ability to stop Sentinels from being called! If you cannot retire your opponent's rear-guard, you can make them discard a card from their hand!
